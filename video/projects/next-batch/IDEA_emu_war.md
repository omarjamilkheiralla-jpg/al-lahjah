# Next idea: The Emu War (1932). Waiting for approval, not scripted yet.

**Hook (A, chosen):** "Australia sent soldiers with machine guns to fight birds... and the birds won."
**Hook (B):** "In 1932, an army went to war with 20,000 birds. The army lost."

**Story beats (house style):** hook -> veterans given poor farmland, drought sends emus into the wheat (setup) -> farmers ask for help; the defence minister sends soldiers with Lewis guns (plan) -> BUT the emus scatter into small groups and stay out of range (the twist) -> THEN they try guns on a truck, which can't aim (conflict) -> "You see," emus are fast and spread out, a gun built for armies can't hit scattered targets (pivot) -> soldiers withdrawn after about a week, and the problem stays (payoff) -> closing line.

**Safe facts** ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emu_War), [Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/topic/Emu-War), [History Hit](https://www.historyhit.com/the-great-emu-war/)):
- 1932, Campion district, Western Australia; drought migration damaging wheat; many farmers were ex-soldiers on marginal land.
- Defence minister Sir George Pearce ordered it; Major G. P. W. Meredith commanded a small team with two Lewis guns; ran 2 Nov to 10 Dec 1932.
- About 20,000 emus (Britannica); the emus split into small groups and stayed out of the guns' effective range; a moving truck was not a stable firing platform.

**Leave out (sources disagree or I could not verify):** exact rounds fired and birds killed (986 vs other figures), the "tanks" quote (no primary source), any parliamentary debate, and any named ornithologist.
