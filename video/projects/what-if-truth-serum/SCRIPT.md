# What if a truth serum leaked into a city's water? (day-by-day what-if)

Status: SCRIPT FOR APPROVAL (nothing built yet).

Words: 458

## Voice-over

### HOOK

What if a truth serum leaked into one city's water?

Everyone who drinks it can only tell the truth.

By Friday, the mayor has quit, the police chief is in jail, and a pizza place has gone out of business.

### MONDAY

Monday, 7 a.m. The water is turned on. The city drinks it with breakfast.

The bus driver says good morning to his first passenger. Then he says, I hate this route. I hate this bus. I hate you.

The barista hands over a coffee. This is yesterday's coffee, she says. I just heat it up.

By lunch, the whole city is whispering the same thing. Something is wrong with the water.

### TUESDAY

Tuesday. The office.

The boss asks his team, who finished the report? Nobody did. Who took my lunch? Everyone looks at Dave. Dave says, yes. It was good.

The new employee is asked, why should we hire you? He says, I need the money.

He gets the job. Because he is the only honest person left in the room.

### WEDNESDAY

Wednesday. The police station.

A line forms outside. Not suspects. Everyone. Hundreds of people, waiting to confess.

I took a candy bar in 1998. I drove home after two drinks. I have been parking in the disabled spot.

Then the police chief walks out, and gets in line too. I take bribes, he says. All of them.

### THURSDAY

Thursday. A wedding.

Do you take this man? The bride looks at him for a long time. No, she says. Not really.

The groom nods. Honestly, he says, me neither.

Fifty guests clap. Somehow, it is the happiest wedding the city has ever seen.

### FRIDAY

Friday. The mayor calls a press conference.

A reporter asks, where did the money go? The mayor tries to say, to the roads. His mouth will not move. He says, to my brother's company.

He tries again. To my brother's boat.

Cameras everywhere. The mayor resigns on the spot. The pizza place closes too, after the owner tells a customer what is really in the sauce.

But then, the scientists arrive.

### SATURDAY

Saturday. The scientists test the water. Every sample. Every pipe.

They find nothing.

No drug. No chemical. Not a drop of serum. It is plain water.

So why did the whole city tell the truth?

### SUNDAY

You see, nobody drugged the water. A rumor started on Monday morning, that it was a truth serum. And everybody believed it.

Psychologists have a name for this. They call it the bogus pipeline. When people think a machine can catch their lies, they confess more.

One rumor. One city. And a week nobody will ever forget.

Tomorrow, the city goes back to normal. The bus driver says, good morning. Everyone says, good morning. And somewhere, a pizza place quietly reopens.
