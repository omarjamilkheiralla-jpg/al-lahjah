# What if every person in one town won a million dollars? (day-by-day what-if)

Status: SCRIPT FOR APPROVAL (nothing built yet).

Words: 472

## Voice-over

### HOOK

What if an entire town won the lottery?

It is a small town of three thousand people. Every week, for ten years, everyone has put in one dollar, to buy a single ticket together.

This week, the numbers match. The jackpot is three billion dollars. Split three thousand ways, that is one million dollars each.

### MONDAY

Monday, 8 a.m. A man in a suit walks into the town hall with a giant check, and a very long list.

Every name in town is on it. The mayor. The baker. The bus driver. The kid who delivers newspapers. One million dollars each.

The town goes silent for exactly three seconds. Then it screams.

### TUESDAY

Tuesday. The shopping spree.

The car dealer sells every car on his lot in one hour. Then he sells the cars that are not even built yet.

A man buys a boat. The town has no lake. He parks it in his driveway, and he is thrilled.

By night, three thousand new trucks are on order. And the only gas station is already running low.

### WEDNESDAY

Wednesday. Everyone quits.

The bus driver quits. The cashier quits. The teacher quits, and so do the plumber, the mechanic, and the only doctor in town.

The baker looks at his ovens. He thinks about sleeping in. He turns off the lights.

But the shops are still open. So everybody goes shopping, at the same time, for the same things.

### THURSDAY

Thursday. The prices.

A loaf of bread was three dollars. Now it is twenty, because everyone has money, and there is only so much bread.

By noon, it is fifty. By dinner, it is one hundred.

A neighbor sells an old couch for ten thousand dollars. A kid sells lemonade for two hundred a cup. And somebody, somewhere, pays it.

### FRIDAY

Friday. The shelves are empty.

The shops can not restock. The truck drivers all quit, and the farms are too far away.

A pipe bursts on Main Street. Nobody can fix it. A plumber would charge one hundred thousand dollars, if one still existed.

The richest town on Earth runs out of bread, eggs, and milk.

### SATURDAY

Saturday. Helicopters arrive with food, and sell it for five hundred dollars a sandwich.

The mayor buys the last one. He eats it in front of the cameras, and cries.

You see, the million dollars was never the problem. Money does not make bread. It only decides who gets it. And when everyone has money, nobody has bread.

### SUNDAY

Economists have a name for this. It is called inflation. Too much money, chasing too few things.

So by Sunday night, the town has made a decision. Everybody goes back to work.

The baker lights his ovens at four in the morning. On his door, a new sign. Help wanted. Pay, one million dollars a day.

Nobody applies. They are all in line for bread.


## Fact notes
- A clearly framed what-if story. No statistics. The one real-world fact is the plain definition of inflation (too much money chasing too few goods). No moral speech: scenes and punchlines only.
