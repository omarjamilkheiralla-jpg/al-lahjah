# "Cut Every Tail" - draft script (about 52 s)

**Idea:** a city paid people for every rat tail, and discovered people would rather farm rats than kill them. Teaches the *perverse incentive* (what you reward is what you get).

**Length:** 176 words, about 53 s at the rulebook pace.

**Title options:** "The City That Paid for Rat Tails" / "Why Hanoi Had More Rats After Paying to Kill Them"

## Voice-over

They cut the tails off rats, then let every single one go.

Why would anyone do that? In 1902, Hanoi was crawling with rats, and its French rulers feared the plague.

So they paid rat catchers for every rat they killed.

But counting dead rats was messy, so the rule changed: just bring us the tail.

Then they opened the offer to everyone: one cent for every rat tail.

Soon, officials noticed something strange. Rats running around with no tails.

You see, the reward was for the tail, not the rat. A rat left alive to breed was worth more than a dead one.

So people cut off the tails and set the rats free. Some even raised rats, just to harvest more tails.

So the rats multiplied, and the French paid for more and more tails. Finally, they cancelled the program.

Economists call this a perverse incentive: pay for the measurement, and you get the measurement, not the result.

The French wanted fewer rats. They paid for tails, and that is exactly what they got.

## Left out on purpose (sources disagree or are thin)
- **Rat counts** (7,985 in one week vs hundreds of thousands in total): numbers vary by source.
- **The exact pay rates** (one cent vs later 2 and 4 cents for sewer crews) and the end date of the bounty.
- **The "cobra effect" in Delhi**: a popular anecdote with weak documentation, so not used as a second story.
- **How many tailless rats there were.** The script only says officials noticed them.

## Wording to watch
- Say "French rulers" or "colonial officials", not "the French people".
- "Terrified" would be too strong; the script says "feared the plague".
- Present the story as what the archives show (historian Michael Vann found the file), not as a verified day-by-day timeline.
