A textbook teaches you to read. A dialect teaches you to belong.

Modern Standard Arabic, الفصحى, is the Arabic of the newspaper and the news bulletin. It is nobody's mother tongue. What people speak at home, in the shop and among friends across the Gulf is something else.

Ask for directions in الفصحى and you will be understood. Ask in اللهجة and you will be answered the way they answer their own.

One sound gives the Gulf away

Where the written language has ك, Emirati often says چ. No textbook teaches it, and every Emirati notices when you get it right.

Four ways in

Every student starts with a free trial class, so we place you by what you can already say, not by what you have already studied.

  1. Stage I

    Foundations

    الأساس

    The sounds, the greetings and the hundred or so phrases that carry an ordinary day. No Arabic assumed.

  2. Stage II

    Conversation

    المحادثة

    Holding your side of a real exchange: the majlis, the shop, the taxi, the corridor at work.

  3. Stage III

    Fluency

    الطلاقة

    Idiom, proverb and humour, and how the register shifts between family, friends and colleagues.

  4. Any stage

    Private & corporate

    خاص ومؤسسي

    One-to-one and team programmes, scheduled around you, on site or online.
